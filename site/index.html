A self-hosted household app

Receipts, shopping lists and spending insights for one household. Self-hosted, phone-friendly.

View on GitHub Changelog

Shared shopping lists

Amounts and units, crossed-off items that come back, live sync between every phone in the household.

Receipts read by AI

Photograph a receipt; the AI (bring your own key) fills in the shop, total and every product line.

Healthy or not

Products are tagged healthy, medium or unhealthy, and fruit and vegetables as seasonal or imported.

Planned vs unplanned

Attach a receipt to a list and see what was bought that was not on it.

A family dashboard

The month's spend, a goal, a trend and the categories behind the numbers.

Five languages

English, Polish, German, French and Spanish, chosen by each person. Reports come out in your language too.

Light, dark, your accent

Five accents and a dark mode, with a simple admin and user split.
